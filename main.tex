\begin{abstract}
We present PHQD, a patch-based hybrid quantum--classical framework that removes Gaussian noise from the latent maps of a convolutional autoencoder on Fashion-MNIST. Overlapping latent patches are denoised by models that combine classical projections with a parameterized quantum circuit. We compare four 4-qubit configurations and a 6-qubit model with data re-uploading against MLP and U-Net baselines. All denoisers improve on the noisy input, but the best classical baseline matches or beats the quantum models, which shows why strong baselines are needed before crediting a quantum circuit.
\end{abstract}
